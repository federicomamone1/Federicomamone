Projects

Research and class projects I've worked on. Each one links to the full paper or file.

NASA Human Lander Challenge (HuLC), 2025

The Cryogenic Complex: fuel tanks for the Moon

Our five-person Jacksonville University team designed the Universal Cryogenic Storage Tank (UCST), a modular, reusable tank for liquid oxygen, liquid hydrogen, and methane. It can sit in a fuel farm on the Moon, dock at a fuel depot in lunar orbit, or clip into a lander like a battery. As the cryogenic storage systems researcher, I worked on tank materials and insulation, built our final presentation deck, and delivered part of the pitch to NASA judges in Huntsville, where we were one of 12 national finalists.

  • National finalist

AVM 102, Fall 2026

Tampa International Airport case study

A research paper on how Florida weather shapes operations at TPA: afternoon thunderstorms, morning fog, and hurricanes. I covered the forecasting tools airports rely on, like METAR, TAF, and terminal Doppler radar, and used Hurricane Ian as a case study. TPA suspended operations on September 27, 2022 and reopened three days later with no serious damage.

  • Airport operations
  • Aviation weather
  • Research

Breeze Airways: regional airline analysis

A full analysis of Breeze Airways, David Neeleman's fifth airline. I looked at its history, its point-to-point network built around underserved cities (about 85% of its routes have no competition), its move to an all-Airbus A220 fleet, and its workforce, safety record, finances, and partnerships.
